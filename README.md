# Customer Segmentation & Marketing Campaign Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer behavior, spending patterns, customer segments, and marketing offer performance using SQL and Tableau Public. The goal is to turn customer and campaign data into actionable business insights.

## Objectives
- Understand customer segments and spending behavior.
- Analyze customer demographics and membership trends.
- Evaluate marketing offers received, viewed, and completed.
- Compare offer engagement and completion rates across customer segments.
- Present key performance indicators through interactive dashboards.

## Tools & Technologies
- **MySQL:** Data cleaning, transformation, and analysis
- **Tableau Public:** Data visualization and dashboard development
- **GitHub:** Project documentation and version control

## Dataset
The project uses customer, offer, and event data. The analysis covers customer profiles, transaction activity, marketing offers, and customer engagement.

## Dashboards

### 1. Executive Marketing Overview
Summarizes key metrics, including total customers, spending, transactions, and marketing offer activity.

### 2. Customer Segmentation Dashboard
Explores customer segment distribution, spending by segment, gender distribution, and customer spending versus transaction counts.

### 3. Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard
Compares offers received, viewed, and completed, and examines offer view and completion rates across customer segments.

### 4. Customer Insights & Demographics Dashboard
Explores age distribution, income distribution, customer membership trends, and average spending by age group.

## Key Findings
- The dataset contains **17,000 customers**.
- Customers recorded **138,953 transactions**.
- There were **76,277 offer-received events**, **57,725 offer-viewed events**, and **33,579 offer-completed events**.
- Customer segments differ in their spending patterns and offer engagement.
- Customer-level average rates and event-count ratios measure different things and should be interpreted separately.

## Business Value
The analysis helps identify customer segments, understand engagement patterns, evaluate campaign activity, and support data-driven marketing decisions.
